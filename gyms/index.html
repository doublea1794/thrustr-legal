Write it like the whiteboard

Thrustr reads it the same way it reads a photo of your board, so the usual shorthand works.

  • Give each part a heading on its own line: Strength, WOD, Accessory.
  • Sets, reps and percents: 5x3 @ 75%, 21-15-9, @ 70-75-80%.
  • Formats: AMRAP 12, EMOM 10, 5 Rounds For Time, Time cap: 15.
  • Rx weights as men/women: Thrusters (95/65).
  • Benchmarks fill themselves in: Fran, Murph.
  • Coaching notes go after Notes: and won't be read as movements.
