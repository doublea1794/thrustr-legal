Thrustr

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Thrustr is a training log for CrossFit and strength athletes. The short version: your workouts stay on your phone, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. If you connect a gym that posts its workouts on Thrustr, that gym can see your name, and if you sign in, your email.

This policy explains what information the Thrustr app ("Thrustr", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. Thrustr is made by Austin Arnold, an individual developer based in Washington, USA.

1. What stays on your phone

Almost everything you put into Thrustr is stored only on your device. We can't see it. This includes:

  • Workouts, sets, reps, weights, times and scores
  • Your 1RMs and their history
  • Your name, gym, class schedule, weekly goal and settings (if you connect a gym on Thrustr, your name is shared with that gym, see section 3)
  • Skills and injuries you choose to note in your profile
  • Files you import from other apps (SugarWOD, Wodify, BTWB)

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Your phone may include it in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's policies cover.

2. Camera and photos

Thrustr asks for camera access to scan your gym's QR code and to read workouts from a whiteboard. If you pick a screenshot or take a photo of a workout, Thrustr first reads the text in it on your phone using Apple's built-in text recognition.

AI reading for signed-in athletes. If you're signed in (see section 5), the photo or screenshot of the workout, resized and with the text your phone read, is also sent through our server to Anthropic, whose AI model reads the workout from it and sends it back as text. This makes handwriting and whiteboards much easier to read. The image is used only for that one read: Thrustr doesn't save it, and it isn't linked to your workouts. We keep a short record that a read happened (your account, the time and whether a workout was found) so each athlete gets a fair daily limit. If you're not signed in, or the AI can't be reached, everything happens on your phone and the image never leaves it. QR code scans always stay on your phone.

In the gym owner view (see section 5), when a gym owner adds the day's workout from a screenshot, the text is read on the phone the same way, and only that text is sent to our server to be tidied into a workout. The screenshot itself never leaves the phone.

3. Finding your gym

When you search for your gym, the search text and your approximate map area are sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. Apple's privacy policy covers that search. Your search text is also checked against the list of gyms that post their workouts on Thrustr. Thrustr only saves the gym you pick, on your phone.

If your gym posts its workouts on Thrustr, the app asks our server for that gym's workouts when you open it. That request contains only the gym's ID, the dates and which classes you take (for example CrossFit, Hyrox or a program your gym added, saved on your phone). The answer includes the gym's workouts and, when the class has been prepped, the weights for each level.

What your gym sees when you connect

When you connect a gym on Thrustr, the app sends our server the name you entered in Thrustr, a random ID made for your copy of the app (not linked to your phone, Apple ID or anything else), and the gym's code if you connected with it. It sends this again each time you open the app, so the gym can see:

  • Your name, as you entered it in Thrustr
  • When you connected, and when you last opened the app
  • Whether you used the gym's code or found the gym in search (people who found it in search are listed as waiting until the gym approves them)

Your workouts, results, maxes, profile and anything else you log are never sent and the gym can't see them. When you disconnect the gym (or switch gyms), the app removes you from that gym's list. The gym can also remove you. Gyms that aren't on Thrustr (found in Apple Maps or added by you) receive nothing.

Preventing abuse

To stop people from listing every gym, guessing gym codes or adding fake members, our server briefly uses the network (IP) address a request comes from to count how often it searches, looks up codes or joins gyms. We keep only the address and a count, and delete them within a day.

4. Notifications

Reminders (like "log your results" after class or "rest is over") are scheduled on your phone. No notification content is sent to us.

If you're signed in and allow notifications, the app sends our server a push token: a random address Apple gives your phone so it can receive notifications. We store it with your account so your gym (or Thrustr) can send you notifications through Apple, for example an announcement from your gym. Signing out removes it, and you can turn notifications off in your phone's Settings at any time.

5. Accounts

Gym owners who post workouts sign in to the Thrustr gym portal with an email and password. We store that email, the gym's name and city, and the workouts they post, with our service provider Supabase, so the gym can manage them and members can see them. Gym owners can ask us to delete their account and gym at any time.

Gym owner view in the app. A gym owner can also sign in to the app's gym owner view with the same email and password. The app sends them to Supabase to sign in and keeps only a sign-in token in the phone's Keychain, never the password. The sign-in only reaches that owner's own gyms. Signing out, or Settings > Erase all data, removes it from the phone.

Member lists. A gym owner can add their gym's member list (names and, optionally, email addresses) to the portal to see which members haven't connected yet. We store it with Supabase only so that gym can see it, and we never contact the people on it. The gym decides whether to invite them, using its own email. If you're on a gym's list and want to be removed, ask your gym or email us.

Coaches. A gym owner can invite coaches from the portal by entering a coach's name. The coach opens the invite link on their phone, and Thrustr turns on coach mode for that gym without an account or password: our server gives that phone a random key, which is kept in the phone's Keychain. We store the coach's name, when they joined and last opened coach mode, a scrambled (hashed) copy of the key, the gym's class schedule with the coach for each class, and class preps, including changes coaches save to them. The key only lets the coach see the day's workout and the gym's classes, and prep their class (make, edit, save or clear the class prep). It doesn't give access to members, member lists or gym settings, and it never shares the coach's own workouts or results. When the owner removes a coach, or the coach leaves coach mode, the key stops working.

AI coach drafts. If a gym uses "Draft with AI" in the portal, the gym's profile (class length, equipment, style and similar notes), its recent posted workouts and the coach's request are sent to Anthropic, which runs the AI model that writes the draft. No athlete information is sent.

Class prep. When a workout is posted, or an owner or coach taps "Prep my class", the workout, the class length, the gym's level names and its profile notes (equipment and things to avoid) are sent to Anthropic to write a short class plan: each part of the workout with a rough time and the weights for each level. When a gym owner adds a workout copied from another app, the copied text is sent to Anthropic to tidy it up. No athlete information is sent. Owners and coaches can edit a class prep by hand or clear it; edits aren't sent to Anthropic. The saved version's weights for each level are shown to the gym's members in the app.

Athlete accounts. Signing in is optional; the app works without an account. If you sign in, in the app or on your gym's member page on the web (thrustr-gyms.vercel.app/g/<gym code>), you choose how:

  • Email: we email you a 6-digit code (and a sign-in link). We store your email address.
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an account identifier and an email address, which can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". The first time, Apple may also share your name, which the app uses only if you didn't enter one.

We store your account with our service provider Supabase: the account identifier, your email address, the name you entered in Thrustr, when the account was made, and which gyms you belong to. Your workouts, results, maxes and profile stay on your phone and are not uploaded.

What your gym sees when you're signed in. In addition to what's listed in section 3, the gym's owner and its front desk staff can see the email address on your account, so they can reach you. If you join a gym on its member page on the web, you're added to that gym's member list with your name and email. You can leave a gym, change your name, sign out or delete your account on that page or in the app.

Gym staff. A gym owner can invite staff to the portal as coaches or front desk. Staff who accept on the website sign in with their own email and password; we store which gym they work at, their role and their email. Coaches can see the gym's workouts, class schedule and class preps. Front desk staff can also see the gym's member list (names, email addresses, when members joined and last opened the app) and approve or remove members. Staff can't see members' workouts, results or maxes.

Results and leaderboards. If you're signed in and confirmed at a gym on Thrustr, when you finish a workout on a day your gym posted one, the app sends that day's result to your gym: each score (for example a time or rounds and reps, and whether it was Rx or scaled), your heaviest set of each lift that day, any PRs from the finish screen, and how hard it felt. It's shown with your name on the day's leaderboard to your gym's staff and its other signed-in members, on the gym's member page, and on the gym's TV page (a private link the gym opens on a screen in the gym, showing today's workout, first names and scores). Members can give fist bumps; we store who bumped which result. The rest of your workouts and history are never sent. Deleting the workout takes that day's result back off the leaderboard, and deleting your account removes all of them.

Announcements. Gym owners and front desk staff can post announcements. We store the title, message, who posted it and who it's for (everyone or one class type). Members see them in the app and on the member page for 30 days, and signed-in members who allow notifications get them as notifications. To send an announcement only to one class type, the app tells your gym which classes you take (for example CrossFit, Hyrox or a program your gym added).

Class booking and check-in. If you're signed in and confirmed at a gym on Thrustr, you can book classes in the app or on your gym's member page. We store each booking (the class, the day, your name, and whether you're booked, on the waitlist, cancelled, or cancelled late by your gym's rules) and each check-in (the class or open gym, the day and time, and how you checked in: the gym's door code, the front desk iPad, or a coach or staff member). When you scan your gym's door code, the app or the member page sends that code with your sign-in so we can check you in. Your gym's owner, coaches and front desk staff see who's booked and checked in for each class, and the owner and front desk see your attendance (visits, last visit, weeks in a row, upcoming bookings, no-shows and late cancels). Your gym's front desk iPad page (a private link the gym opens on its own device) shows the names of members booked for the current classes and lets members find their name to check in. If you move up from a waitlist, we send you a notification if you allowed them. Deleting your account removes your bookings. Your gym keeps its record of past check-ins (your name and the dates) for its attendance history, no longer linked to your account; email us to have those removed too.

Waivers. If your gym uses Thrustr for its waiver, you sign it in the app or on your gym's member page by typing your full name (a parent or guardian can sign for someone under 18). We store the waiver version and its exact text, the name you typed, who you signed for and your relationship to them, the date and time, and your device's IP address and app or browser details, so the gym has a record of the signature. Your gym's owner, coaches and front desk staff can see and print signed waivers. Because a signed waiver is a record your gym may need to keep, deleting your account doesn't delete your signatures: they stay with your gym, no longer linked to your account. Email us if you want them removed and we'll check with your gym.

Member profiles. Your gym's owner and front desk staff can see a profile for each member: your name, when you joined and last opened the app, your email address (if you signed in or the gym added it to its member list), your attendance, bookings, waiver status, recent gym workout results, and notes and a plan description the gym's staff write about you. Coaches see the same without your email. If you choose to share your injuries or limitations with your gym (in the app under Skills and injuries, or on the member page), its staff see that text until you stop sharing.

Messages from your gym. Your gym's owner and front desk staff can send email and notifications to groups of members (for example everyone, new members, members who haven't signed the waiver, or members who haven't been in recently). Emails come from your gym "via Thrustr", replies go to the staff member who sent it, and every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn your gym's emails on or off on its member page. We keep a copy of each message the gym sends (subject, text, who it was for and how many it reached). Your gym can turn off the automatic notification that tells you when you move up from a waitlist.

Activity log. For each gym, our server records who changed what in the portal and the app (for example "posted a workout", "approved a member", "invited front desk staff"), with the time, the person's role and a short label such as a member's name or a workout's date. Only the gym's owner can see it. It doesn't record what was written, only which fields changed.

We use account data only to sign you in, to link you to your gym, to book classes and check you in, to record what you buy from your gym, to send notifications you allowed, and to connect your account to a subscription if you buy one.

6. Payments

Paying your gym. If your gym takes payments through Thrustr, you can buy its memberships, class packs and drop-ins in the app or on its member page. You pay on Stripe's secure page, and the money goes to your gym's own Stripe account: your gym is the seller. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by Thrustr. To set you up as a customer on your gym's Stripe account, we send Stripe your name and email. We store a record of what you buy with your account: the plan, its price, its status (for example active, cancelling or ended), renewal and end dates, the reason you gave if you cancel (optional), and each payment's amount, date, status and receipt link. Your gym's owner and front desk staff can see these records, and your gym also sees your payments in its own Stripe account. Thrustr receives a small fee from each payment, paid by your gym. Because payment records are financial records your gym and Stripe have to keep, deleting your Thrustr account doesn't delete them: they stay with your gym, no longer linked to your account. Payment emails. If a payment fails, we email you (through Resend) to update your card, and your gym's owner or front desk can send you one reminder a day while it's unpaid. Your gym's owner gets an email when a payment fails or a member cancels, with the member's name, the plan, the amount and any cancel reason. We record when these emails were sent so they aren't sent twice. Once a day we also check each gym's records against Stripe and fix anything that was missed. Booking rules and fees. We use your purchases to work out what you can book (for example, classes left this week) and record which purchase each booking used. If your gym requires a plan to book, it can mark you as paying outside Thrustr. If your gym sets late-cancel or no-show fees, we record each fee (your name, the class, the amount and whether it was charged or waived). Your card is saved with your gym's Stripe account so your gym's owner can charge a fee; Thrustr never sees your card details, and we email you a receipt for any fee charged. Your gym's owner and front desk staff see these records and a "Payment failed" flag on class rosters when a membership payment fails. Pauses, plan changes and refunds. If you ask your gym to pause your membership, we store the dates, your reason (optional), and your gym's answer and note, and we email the owner about your request and you about the answer. We also record plan changes your gym makes, refunds (amount, date and an optional note from your gym, which we email to you), whether you used a free trial or a new-member offer (so they're once per person), and how many times each of your gym's coupon codes was used. Coupon codes are entered on Stripe's checkout page.

Gyms getting paid. A gym owner who sets up payments creates their own Stripe account on Stripe's sign-up pages, and Stripe collects the business, identity and bank details it needs; Thrustr doesn't see them. We store the gym's Stripe account ID and whether it can take payments and receive payouts, plus the gym's plans and its cancel rules.

Thrustr paid plans. Thrustr doesn't sell a plan of its own today. If we offer one in the future, payments are processed by Stripe the same way, and we receive your subscription status (for example, active or canceled), the plan, and renewal dates so the app can unlock paid features.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use third-party analytics in the app.

8. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help run Thrustr, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in provider, which also sends sign-in emails. Gym portal accounts, posted workouts, gym member lists, the names of athletes who connected a gym, athlete accounts (email, account identifier, name and gym memberships), push tokens, staff names, roles and sign-ins, class schedules (times, class types and coaches), level names, class preps, each gym's activity log, gym owner sign-ins from the portal and the app, a record of each AI workout read (account, time and whether a workout was found, never the image), gyms' plans, members' purchase and payment records, and subscription records
  • Anthropic: writes AI workout drafts and class preps, tidies workouts gym owners copy in (in the app or on the portal), and reads workouts from the whiteboard photos and screenshots signed-in athletes scan
  • Stripe: processes payments to gyms (your name, your email and what you buy go to your gym's Stripe account) and gym owners' Stripe sign-up, and would process a Thrustr paid plan if one is offered
  • Apple: App Store, Sign in with Apple, Maps search, push notifications (Apple Push Notification service delivers them to your phone) and the Keychain that keeps your sign-in, a coach's key or a gym owner's sign-in on their phone
  • Vercel: hosts the gym portal and members' gym pages on the web
  • Resend: delivers emails gyms send to their members and payment emails (your email address, the gym's name and the message)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

You can erase everything Thrustr has saved on your phone at any time by deleting the app, or in the app under Settings: Delete account if you're signed in, or Erase all data if you're not. Delete account (in the app or on your gym's member page) deletes your account, removes you from every gym's member list, removes your push tokens and your staff access, and erases what's on your phone. To be removed from a gym's member list without deleting your account, disconnect or leave the gym, or email us. Gym owners delete their gym on the website before deleting their account. Activity log entries are kept while the gym exists and deleted with it. Records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons (such as payment records held by Stripe) are kept only as long as required.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Most of your data is on your phone and under your control already. For anything we hold, email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when the app talks to our servers, and we limit access to account data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Children

Thrustr isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Future features

We plan to add features like backup and an AI coach. Before any feature sends new kinds of data off your phone, we'll update this policy and the app will tell you what's shared.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app.
